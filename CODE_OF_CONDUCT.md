# Code of conduct

graphing is a small project made by people who like drawing things clearly. We want it to be a good place to ask questions, share diagrams and send patches, whoever you are and however much you know.

## The short version

Be kind, be patient, and assume good intent.

## What that looks like

- **Help people get unstuck.** Everyone was new to Rust, gpui, SysML or BPMN once. Explain things; don't make people feel slow for asking.
- **Critique the work, not the person.** "This breaks undo when the group is empty" is useful. "Who wrote this?" is not.
- **Disagree in good faith.** Say what you think and why, listen to the other side, and accept that some calls go the other way.
- **Respect people's time.** Clear bug reports, small focused pull requests and patient reviews go a long way.
- **Give credit.** If someone's idea or code helped, say so.

## What is not okay

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Discrimination or slurs of any kind.
- Repeatedly pushing on someone who has asked you to stop.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.

## If something goes wrong

Most friction is a misunderstanding, and a calm word in the thread fixes it.

When it is more than that, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/Toyz), or use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment or issue. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people from the project. These steps are used as little as needed, and only to keep the space welcoming.

## Where this applies

Issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.
